# PROJECT — AI Data Guardian

## One-line

An AI Trust Platform that scores, explains, and remediates the trustworthiness and AI-readiness
of data for a specific use case, demonstrable in under 60 seconds.

## Why it exists

Governance and quality tools produce scattered findings but few executive conclusions. Expertise
sits with a few people and does not scale. Business leaders do not see the link between a data
defect and an operational decision. This product turns Data Office expertise into a reusable AI
capability that evaluates trust, explains business exposure, and recommends prioritized actions.

## Success is measured by

The reaction of a decision-maker during a short demo:
> "We thought we would see a data-control tool. We now see a trust capability that secures
> decisions and AI agents."

## Primary users

Executive committee, business/KPI owners, data owners, data stewards, compliance leaders,
Data & AI Office.

## Guiding principles

- Business consequence first; one key message per view.
- Facts, assumptions, and simulations are always visually distinct.
- Deterministic core; generative explanation layer on top.
- Simple, replaceable components; traceable decisions.
- Demonstration-first, synthetic data by default.

## Scope posture

Build a demonstration-oriented MVP on files and simulated connectors, with a modular architecture
that later allows swapping synthetic data for real integrations. No production writes, no
autonomous data modification, no legally binding compliance engine in the MVP.
